ZYRORA — FRAME 01

ABOUT ZYRORA

A platform built
for intelligent
problem-solving.

As artificial intelligence began reshaping how the world builds, TENSAI asked a simple question: what if students didn't just study AI, but shaped it? ZYRORA is the answer — a flagship hackathon designed to turn that curiosity into intelligent, working solutions.

Over 24 hours, participants from colleges across the country come together to design, build, and ship real AI-driven products — guided by mentors, judged by industry leaders, and fuelled by a shared belief that the best ideas deserve to be built.

ABOUT TENSAI

The Student
Technical Association.

TENSAI exists to make technical excellence a student-led pursuit — built on community, research, and the belief that the best way to learn technology is to build with it.

What began as a small circle of students has grown into the institution's driving force for innovation — a community where research, coding, and curiosity are treated as everyday habits, not occasional events.

TENSAI — FRAME 01

Student-Led Innovation

Every initiative, workshop, and event is conceived and run by students — for students.

Technical Communities

Focused groups across AI, systems, and design where members learn from one another.

Research Culture

Encouraging students to read, question, and publish — treating curiosity as a discipline.

Workshops

Hands-on sessions that turn theory into working code, models, and prototypes.

Hackathons

Time-boxed challenges — like ZYRORA — that push ideas from concept to build.

Industry Collaboration

Leadership

Emerging Technologies
